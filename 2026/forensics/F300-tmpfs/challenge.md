[ FILE REF: S2E1 "THE CONTINGENCY" // SAFEHOUSE ASSET - MEMORY ACQUISITION ]

He waited in the safehouse for instructions. We took it eleven seconds after his terminal lit up. He was at the keyboard. He saw us coming. He had time to type three commands and reach for the keyboard one more time before the door opened.

He erased what he could see. He erased it from disk. He erased it from the directory listing. He believed he had succeeded because his screen showed it gone.

He did not understand that 'erased' is not what he thought. He did not understand that the process holding his file was still alive, and the kernel was still tracking the pages, and the memory had not yet been recycled.

The disk image is empty. The memory is not.

Find what he kept. Find what he was about to send. Find who he is.

- THE MACHINE
