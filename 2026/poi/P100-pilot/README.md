## Summary

A new number, no context. What surfaced with them says they are dangerous, and the Machine is not sure the evidence is honest. Read the dossier, not just what it says, and assemble their nine-digit designation.

## Skills

- reading image EXIF and PDF document properties
- recovering text hidden under a redaction
- assembling an identifier from pieces across several files
- questioning evidence that is too convenient

## Tools that help

`exiftool`, `pdfinfo` and `pdftotext`, a text viewer.
