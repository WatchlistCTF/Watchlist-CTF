# P450: Endgame

**Category:** PoI · **Points:** 450

```text
[ FILE REF: S3E8 "ENDGAME" // THE CALL ]

You found her. You caught the witness lying. Now you finish it.

Something is coming, and it is coming soon. I gave you a number at
the start and told you the evidence looked dishonest. It was. The
person the evidence wanted you to fear is the person it was built
to bury.

Put it together. All of it. The cache she carried, the records, the
lie. Tell me who is about to commit a crime, and where, and when.

Give me the number. Not the one I started with. The right one.

I will do the rest.

  - THE MACHINE
```
