# P250: Witness

**Category:** PoI · **Points:** 250

```text
[ FILE REF: S1E7 "WITNESS" // TESTIMONY RECEIVED ]

Someone came forward. Her manager, Daniel Pierce. He is calm, he is
specific, and he is certain: she stalked him, she threatened him,
she invented the evidence she's been carrying. He has proof.

It is a good story. It fits everything you already believe.

I do not deal in stories. I deal in records, and records do not
care what anyone is certain of. He gave me times. Check them.

Find where the story and the clock disagree.
```
