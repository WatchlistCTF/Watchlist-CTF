# O250: Manifest

**Category:** OSINT · **Points:** 350

```text
[ FILE REF: "MANIFEST" // METRO AIRSPACE - ONE WEEK OF EXHAUST ]

Aircraft are required to announce themselves. Position, altitude,
heading - broadcast in the clear, once a second, to anyone with an
antenna. It is the law. The people who fly the interesting missions
know this. They forget it anyway.

I pulled a week of one metro's traffic. Most of it is nothing -
couriers, charters, the same six business jets flying the same
boring triangles.

Twice this week, an aircraft did something it did not file. It went
somewhere, and then it went in circles. Once on Monday. Once on
Thursday. Two different tails. They think that makes them two
different stories.

It does not. They were looking at the same thing.

Find both. Prove they watched the same ground. Tell me what was
underneath them - the coordinate is not on any list I gave you,
because someone made sure it wouldn't be.

  - THE MACHINE

  Files: fleet_week_20260914-20260920.json  (the week of traffic)
         sites.csv                          (known facilities in the metro)
         registry.json                      (grid registry - keyed by location)
```
